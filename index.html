The model

A puzzle becomes a graph.

A cube configuration can be encoded as a vertex. Applying any legal face turn creates a neighboring vertex, connected by an edge. Solving the cube means finding a path from the scrambled vertex to the solved one.

The highlighted nodes are the computed solution states. The dim branches show a representative sample of other legal moves available at each step, making the size and branching structure of the search space visible.
